    Prepare an opt-in request

    This form creates a copyable JSON snippet on your device only. It is not sent to anyone, does not update the directory, and does not enroll you. No approved private intake channel is configured, so do not enter real employee details.

    There is no real employee opt-in or opt-out process in this prototype. Before real profiles are published, a private intake and removal process must be approved; removal would require updating and republishing the JSON file.
